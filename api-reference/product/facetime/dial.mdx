<Note>
    Only customers who lease numbers that support FaceTime Audio can use this endpoint.
</Note>

<Warning>
    Phone numbers must be in E.164 format with country codes. `to` can also be a FaceTime email.
</Warning>

Dialing creates a call in `queued` and fires [`facetime.call.initiated`](/api-reference/webhook/example-v2#facetime-call-initiated). When the remote side starts ringing, status becomes `ringing` and Contiguity fires [`facetime.call.ringing`](/api-reference/webhook/example-v2#facetime-call-ringing).

If you omit `from`, Contiguity picks one of your leased FaceTime Audio numbers at random. `caller_id` is optional and is presented as `Maybe: caller_id` to the recipient. If not provided, it will be a formatted version of your leased number, e.g., `Maybe: +1 (415) 555-1234`

The response is the same call object returned by [Get a call](/api-reference/product/facetime/get). At dial time `link` is `null`, `participants` is `[]`, and `duration` is `null`.
