Every iMessage falls into one of two buckets: you're replying to someone who texted you first (inbound-first), or you're the one starting the conversation (outbound-first).
Which one you pick for a given use case is the single biggest factor in how risky that message looks to Apple's detection systems.
Most real products use both, just for different things.

## Hybrid: what most products actually do

Use outbound-first sparingly, for messages the customer already expects: a reminder for an appointment they booked, a shipping update for an order they placed. Use inbound-first for everything else, especially first contact.

It's also worth remembering that once a customer has replied to you, further messages in that thread aren't cold outbound anymore. A shipping update sent to someone who texted you last week about their order is a reply in an ongoing conversation, not a blast to a stranger.

<Tip>
  A good rule of thumb: if the recipient would be surprised to hear from you, treat it as outbound-first and follow the [outbound-first](/kb/compliance/imessage/strategies/outbound-first) guidance. If they're expecting it, or they've talked to you recently, it's safe to treat as a reply.
</Tip>
